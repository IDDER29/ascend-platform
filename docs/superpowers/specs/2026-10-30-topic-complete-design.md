# Topic complete

The page after a topic's last lesson (`/level-complete/[level]`), rebuilt from real records.

- **Finished:** the clan crest, "B-rank task done on <date>", "<Topic>, finished.", the clan joined, lessons and days taken. Real scores: lessons, this topic's Practice cards held solid, share right on them. What finishing earned (clan, topic badge, the headband if this topic won it) and how far it takes the next title. Next: the next topic's first lesson, or ShellForge after the last topic.
- **Not finished:** no celebration; how many lessons are done and the ones still to do, with Continue.
- No levels or "unlocked" language; nothing locks. Coach colours (no purple).
- Model: `src/lib/achievements/topic-complete-model.ts` (tested). Guests see the development sample like /achievements.
- The old version, with made-up numbers, is kept at `/level-completev1`.
